# Booking sessions

People share the cost of a venue booked by one person. Participants can take a
place, wait for an opening, or arrange a named replacement when they leave.

## Language

**Booking Room**:
The shared coordination space for a session at an already-booked venue,
bringing together its booker and participants.

**Participant list**:
The participation records associated with one booking room, including people
who have joined and past changes to their participation.
_Avoid_: Roster

**Member list**:
The users who belong to a group, including its owner.
_Avoid_: Membership roster

**Booker**:
The person who books the venue for a session and takes the initial booking risk.

**Participant**:
A person seeking or holding a place in a session.

**Booking share**:
The portion of a session's booking cost put on hold when a participant commits.

**Early withdrawal**:
A participant's withdrawal at least 30 hours before the session starts,
entitling them to an immediate refund of their held booking share.

**Late withdrawal**:
A participant's withdrawal less than 30 hours before the session starts, with
their share still held while they await a replacement.

**Awaiting replacement**:
The position of a late-withdrawing participant whose share remains held until
a replacement is found or the session is cancelled. Without a replacement when
the session starts, that share is forfeited.

**Payout attempt**:
One attempt to pay the booker from a session's held funds.

**Joining waitlist**:
The ordered queue of people interested in taking an ordinary place when one
becomes available.

**Awaiting refund group**:
Withdrawn participants whose held shares have not yet been refunded or
forfeited, distinct from people waiting to join.

**Personal replacement**:
The one named person invited to take a particular withdrawing participant's
one place.

**Reserved place**:
A withdrawing participant's place held for their named personal replacement,
unavailable to ordinary admission while that invitation remains active.

**Successful replacement**:
A replacement who has successfully joined with their full booking share held.
An agreement alone is not a successful replacement.

**Replacement link**:
A link to an invitation for one reserved place that only its named invitee can
accept.

**Open-slot replacement**:
A participant who fills an ordinary opening without targeting a specific
withdrawing participant.

**Offering a personal place to the waitlist**:
Making a previously reserved place available for ordinary admission through the
session's group/public access rules and joining waitlist.
