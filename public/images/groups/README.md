# Group card artwork

`racket-court.webp` was generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this page, then resized to 1200px wide and encoded as WebP. Covers are decorative illustrations of sport and do not represent uploaded group photos.

Prompt:

> Create a single photorealistic editorial photograph for a sports group's website card cover, landscape 16:9 composition. A classic unbranded black and cream tennis racket rests diagonally on a muted olive green outdoor hard court, with one fuzzy yellow-green tennis ball next to the racket head. White painted court line runs diagonally below. Warm late-afternoon golden sunlight and broad dappled tree shadows, cinematic yet natural documentary photography, slightly desaturated earth palette, detailed surface texture, calm premium local tennis club feeling, no people, no text, no logos, no borders, no collage. Camera at a low oblique overhead angle. Racket and ball centered slightly to right; fill the full landscape image with the court and shadows, no blank margins. Use enough depth of field for the racket and ball to be sharp.

The other covers reuse existing court photography at `/images/sessions/court.webp`, `/images/session-court.webp`, and `/images/sports/tennis.jpg`.
